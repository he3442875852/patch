Patch Border Options

Border choice affects shape precision, thickness, durability and the final visual style.

Merrowed border

A classic stitched edge for many round, square and simple-shape embroidered patches.

Heat-cut edge

A clean cut edge for custom outlines and thinner patch profiles.

Laser-cut edge

A precise edge option for detailed outlines and woven or leather labels.

Raised PVC border

A molded raised border for PVC patches that need depth and structure.
