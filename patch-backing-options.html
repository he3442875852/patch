Patch Backing Options

Backing affects how a patch is applied, removed, washed, packed and priced.

Sew-on

Durable for jackets, uniforms, bags, denim and long-term wear.

Iron-on

Heat-activated backing for suitable fabrics, with sewing recommended for high-wear items.

Hook and loop

Removable backing for morale patches, tactical gear, uniforms and bags.

Self-adhesive

Peel-and-stick backing for temporary use, packaging, displays and events.

Pin backing

Removable option for badges, events and retail display.

No backing

Used when a sewing team or garment factory will apply the patch later.
