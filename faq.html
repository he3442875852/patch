Custom Patch FAQ

Use these answers to prepare a clearer quote request and choose a practical production method.

Which patch type should I choose?

Embroidery is classic, woven supports fine detail, PVC is durable, chenille is plush, leather gives a label feel and printed patches support complex color artwork.

Can I upload artwork?

Yes. The quote form accepts JPG, JPEG, PNG, PDF, AI, SVG and EPS files up to 8MB.

Can I approve a proof before production?

Yes. Proof approval helps confirm layout, material, color, border and backing before bulk production.

Do you support hook and loop backing?

Yes. Hook and loop backing is available for removable patches, uniforms, morale patches, bags and gear.
