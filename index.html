How the order process works

Clear Approval Steps Before Production

1

Send an Inquiry

Share artwork or logo, patch type, finished size, quantity, backing, intended application, shipping country and delivery requirement.

2

Receive a Quotation

HeyPalPatch reviews design, size, colors, quantity, material, backing, border and shipping requirements before preparing a quotation.

3

Place the Order

After the quotation is confirmed, PayPal payment is available for the confirmed order. PayPal account details are not published on the website.

A digital proof is usually prepared within approximately 3-4 days after order details and artwork requirements are confirmed.

Production is arranged after the customer approves the digital proof. Timing is usually approximately 8-10 days and may vary by order.

Standard MOQ

MOQ, Samples and Timing

Minimum Order Quantity

The standard minimum order quantity is 50 pieces for embroidered patches and 100 pieces for most other patch types. Final MOQ may still depend on the design, size, material and production requirements.

Physical Samples

Physical samples are available with a sample fee. The fee depends on artwork, finished size, number of colors, material, backing and border requirements.

Proof and Production Timing

A digital proof is usually prepared within approximately 3-4 days after details are confirmed. Bulk production usually takes approximately 8-10 days after customer approval.
