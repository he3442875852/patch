Custom Patch Size Guide

Patch size controls readability, texture, cost and practical placement.

Hat patch

Often about 2.25 to 3 inches, depending on crown shape and artwork.

Chest patch

Often about 3 to 4 inches for apparel, uniforms and small merchandise.

Shoulder patch

Often about 3.5 to 4.5 inches for uniforms and jackets.

Large back patch

Often 8 inches and larger, with proof review needed for readability.

Sizes are reference ranges only. Final dimensions should follow the approved proof and placement requirements.
