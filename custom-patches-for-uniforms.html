Custom Patches for Uniforms

Uniform patches often require consistent size, readable text, durable backing and repeatable color references. Embroidered and woven patches are common, while PVC may suit removable hook-and-loop systems. Best Fit

Uniform patches usually require consistent sizing, readable identifiers and a backing that matches how the garment will be worn and maintained.

FAQ

Common Buyer Questions

Answers below use only confirmed HeyPalPatch order information: standard MOQ, paid samples, proof approval, PayPal payment, shipment photos and international shipping coordination from China.

  • 50-piece MOQ for embroidered patches
  • 100-piece MOQ for most other patch types
  • Digital proof before bulk production

What is the minimum order quantity?

The standard MOQ is 50 pieces for embroidered patches and 100 pieces for most other patch types. Final requirements may depend on the design, size and production method.

Do you provide physical samples?

Yes. Physical samples are available with a sample fee. The fee depends on the artwork, size, number of colors, material and production requirements.

How long does a digital proof take?

A digital proof is usually prepared within approximately 3-4 days after the order information and artwork requirements are confirmed.

How long does bulk production take?

Bulk production usually takes approximately 8-10 days after the customer approves the digital proof. Timing may vary depending on the patch type, quantity and artwork complexity.

Will I approve the design before production?

Yes. The customer reviews and approves the digital proof before bulk production is arranged.
