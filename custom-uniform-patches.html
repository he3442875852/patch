HeyPalPatch Guide

Custom Uniform Patches

Uniform patches need durable materials, readable artwork and practical backing choices.

Embroidered Patches

Embroidered patches use stitched thread over a twill or fabric base, giving logos a classic raised texture and durable edge finish.

Explore

PVC Patches

PVC patches are molded rubber patches with clean color separation, dimensional layers and excellent durability for outdoor and gear applications.

Explore

Woven Patches

Woven patches use fine yarn to create a flatter and smoother surface than embroidery, making them useful for small text and detailed artwork.

Explore

Chenille Patches

Chenille patches use plush loop yarn over a felt base for a bold varsity and fashion look, especially on large letters and numbers.

Explore

Leather Patches

Leather patches create a retail label look using genuine leather or PU leather, with embossed, debossed or laser engraved artwork.

Explore

Printed Patches

Printed patches use a flat printed textile or sublimation surface, making them suitable for gradients, photographs and complex illustration.
