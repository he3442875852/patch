Material Fit

Artwork Strengths

Best for bold logos, readable lettering and simple color blocks. Very small text, gradients and photo details may need woven or printed patches instead.

Surface Feel

Raised thread, visible stitch direction and a classic tactile surface.

Design Watchouts

This option is not ideal for photo-realistic artwork, very tiny lettering, smooth gradients. Send the final artwork and size so readability and production requirements can be checked before quotation.

MOQ, Samples, Proofs and Timing

The standard minimum order quantity is 50 pieces. Final MOQ may still depend on design, size, material and production requirements.

Physical Samples

Physical samples are available with a sample fee. The fee depends on artwork, finished size, number of colors, material, backing and border requirements.

A digital proof is usually prepared within approximately 3-4 days after details are confirmed. Bulk production usually takes approximately 8-10 days after customer approval.
