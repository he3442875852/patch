Flexible molded rubber

Custom PVC Patches

PVC patches are molded rubber patches with clean color separation, dimensional layers and excellent durability for outdoor and gear applications.

Request a QuoteView Patch Styles

Material Characteristics

  • Flexible molded rubber
  • Flat 2D and raised 3D layers
  • Waterproof and outdoor suitable
  • Raised PVC border or sew channel

Best For

  • Outdoor gear
  • Tactical uniforms
  • Bags
  • Morale patches

Available Backings

  • Hook and loop
  • Sew channel
  • Self-adhesive
  • No backing

Border Options

  • Raised PVC border
  • Heat-cut edge
Artwork Guidance

Prepare Art Around the Material.

Use clear outlines and separated color areas. Photographic gradients usually fit printed patches better than molded PVC.

Common Sizes
  • Gear patch: about 2.5 to 3.5 in
  • Shoulder patch: about 3 to 4 in
  • Large badge: about 4 to 5 in

Sizes are reference ranges only. Final dimensions should follow your approved proof.

FAQ

Questions About PVC Patches

Are PVC patches waterproof?

PVC patches are waterproof and suitable for outdoor use when the backing and attachment method match the product.

Can I request proof approval?

Yes. Proof approval is used to confirm shape, color, material, border and backing before bulk production.
