Artwork Guidance

Prepare Art Around the Material.

Separate genuine leather and PU leather requirements early because color, texture, backing and edge behavior can differ.

Common Sizes
  • Cap patch: about 2 to 3 in
  • Denim patch: about 2.5 to 4 in
  • Bag label: about 2 to 4 in

Sizes are reference ranges only. Final dimensions should follow your approved proof.

FAQ

Questions About Leather Patches

Do leather patches use genuine leather or PU leather?

Both are available. Genuine leather and PU leather should be selected separately because they have different texture, pricing and application behavior.

Can I request proof approval?

Yes. Proof approval is used to confirm shape, color, material, border and backing before bulk production.
